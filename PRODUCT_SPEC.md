# Product specification — foundation v1

## Outcome-first curriculum

Final task: explain delivery delays with reproducible evidence and at least one limitation. Work backward: evaluate claims → summarize and compare groups → handle missingness → inspect variables → formulate a testable hypothesis. Later extend to retail inventory and air quality using original seeded synthetic data. Learners investigate, predict, experiment, observe, explain, validate and adapt.

Track content defines six chapters with two lesson previews each: analytical thinking, Python, cleaning, EDA, statistics and introductory ML. Full V1 later requires 36 validated quiz questions, 12 executable challenges and three complete investigations; none are claimed as finished in Phase 1.

## Public pattern review

These are general principles, not copied content or interfaces. No assets or lessons were reused.

| Source | Public pattern / evidence | DATUM design decision |
|---|---|---|
| [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/interactive-learning) | In-browser coding exercises reduce setup friction. | Run practice locally in a worker in Phase 4; preserve explicit execution states. |
| [Kaggle Learn Pandas](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/pandas) | Focused topic course; Python prerequisite. The main Learn page did not expose readable content during review. | Short, connected topic lessons with explicit prerequisites; avoid claiming detailed competitor interaction findings. |
| [Dataquest](https://www.dataquest.io/) | Skill/career paths and practical project organization. | Connect a sequenced track to a realistic investigation outcome. |
| [Codecademy](https://www.codecademy.com/) | Skill-oriented course discovery and practice-led learning. | Keep navigation and next actions clear, with actual demonstrated evidence replacing visit-based progress. |
| [Brilliant](https://brilliant.org/) | Official site timed out twice during inspection. Detailed pattern validation remains incomplete. | Prediction-before-reveal comes from the user’s product brief; do not attribute unverified mechanisms to Brilliant. |

## Phase 1 acceptance mapping

Dashboard: clearly labeled sample learner; continue card; mission preview; progress; skills; recent activity; recommended hypothesis exercise. Learn: chapter overview and search, unique lesson routes, temporary reflection and authored guidance. Investigate: mission checklist, dataset/chart/code views and offline mentor panel. Practice/Quiz/Skills/Notebook: informative empty/upcoming states, no pretend launch or save buttons. Settings: working theme preference and persistence disclosure. Errors: unknown route, unknown lesson, unavailable browser storage and render-error recovery. Mobile: inline menu and selectable workspace panels. Keyboard: native links/buttons, explicit labels, focus rings and skip link.

Future progress must be earned by deterministic evidence, not page visits. Do not label learners job-ready or score intelligence. Browser practice checks are unverified. Authenticated assessments need private server-side keys.
